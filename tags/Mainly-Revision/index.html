Mainly Revision
zh 長夜集

In China's middle school English textbook, there are some units called 'mainly revision', designed to review what has been learned in the previous chapters. At that time when I was a student, the words 'mainly' and 'revision' were beyond my English vocabulary although I had to encounter them every once in a while. Now when I write my personal stories, that strange 'mainly revision' suddenly comes to me. Surely I know their meaning now, just as I understand some of my past experiences that I failed to grasp when they happened. The former name of this project, 'memoir', did hit the nail on the head: it tells my life stories, as well as my humanity, but the moment those strange words from over twenty years ago unexpectedly struck me, it got this new name. By it I mean, we need this 'mainly revision' now and then, after several chapters in our lives being taught. So let me do it here.
